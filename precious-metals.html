Compliance framework

Every transaction — KYC · AML · sanctions screened

AYYS integrates compliance at the transaction level. Onboarding, counterparty screening and trade documentation meet OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains.

KYC / AML
Full beneficial ownership verification. UBO chain documented to natural person level. Enhanced DD for high-risk jurisdictions.
Sanctions
OFAC, EU, UN and UK sanctions screening on all counterparties, vessels, flags, ports and transit corridors prior to any commitment.
OECD DD
Conflict minerals due diligence aligned with OECD 5-step framework. Origin documentation mandatory for all sensitive corridor flows.
EU CBAM
Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism reporting support for applicable metal imports into the EU. Embedded CO₂ data available on request.
