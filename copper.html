Copper scrap · secondary grades

Certified scrap sourcing — grades 94 to 99%

AYYS sources certified copper scrap across ISRI-graded categories from collection networks in Eastern Europe, Turkey and the Levant. All lots undergo independent pre-shipment assay. Pricing on LME discount basis or fixed spread per agreed grade. Minimum lot 20 mt.

ISRI — Birch / Cliff
Bare bright copper wire
Uncoated, unalloyed solid copper wire. No solder, paint, insulation or tinning. Minimum gauge 16 AWG. No hair wire, burnt wire or copper-clad.
Cu ≥ 99.0%
ISRI — Berry
Copper clippings & punchings
Clean copper clippings, punchings and bus bar from electrical manufacturing. Free of oil, grease, solder and excessive oxidation. Baled or loose.
Cu ≥ 98.0%
ISRI — Candy
Mixed unalloyed copper scrap
Miscellaneous copper scrap including pipe, tubing, bus bar, commutator segments. Free from excessive iron, lead or other base metals. Unalloyed.
Cu ≥ 96.0%
ISRI — Clove
Light copper — alloyed
Miscellaneous low-grade copper including alloyed items. Copper tubing with fittings, coils with small amounts of solder or brass content. Assay required.
Cu ≥ 94.0%
ISRI — Druid
Copper wire nodules — granulated
Copper wire nodules from granulation of insulated copper wire. Clean copper granules, free of ferrous metals. Density ≥ 7.8 g/cm³. Assay mandatory.
Cu ≥ 97.5%
Heavy melt — machined
Copper turnings & borings
Copper and copper alloy turnings, borings and milling swarf from machining. Dry, oil-free, clean. Assay-based pricing; umpire clause applies to all lots.

Compliance framework

Every copper transaction — KYC · AML · sanctions screened

AYYS integrates compliance at the transaction level. Onboarding, counterparty screening and trade documentation meet OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains and EU CBAM requirements where applicable.

KYC / AML
Full beneficial ownership verification. UBO chain documented to natural person level. Enhanced DD for high-risk jurisdictions including DRC and Iran.
Sanctions
OFAC, EU, UN and UK sanctions screening on all counterparties, vessels, flags, ports and transit corridors prior to any commitment or instruction.
OECD DD
Conflict minerals due diligence aligned with OECD 5-step framework. Origin documentation and chain-of-custody mandatory for all DRC and Zambia copper flows.
EU CBAM
Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism reporting support for copper cathode and rod imports into the EU. Embedded CO₂ intensity data available on request.
